**Some of the features of Sol-IDE:**

- Sol-IDE works on Mobile, that means you can write, run and test Solidity Smart Contracts on your phone.
- Sol-IDE works well with all major wallets. It also allows you to test using virtual wallets, it is a bring your wallet-first platform.
- Sol-IDE works with all major blockchains. It provides support for 67 major blockchains and that number also looks to grow rapidly.
- Sol-IDE is built on modern technologies, that means more optimized performance and better user experience, with a captivating UI that does not strain your eye.
- Sol-IDE is built for the unreached 6 billion crypto users and devs.
- Sol-IDE is built to quickly onboard people to the world of Blockchain Programming.
