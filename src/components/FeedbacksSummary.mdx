**SendFeedbacks is an AI-powered feedback collection and management platform designed to help businesses gather insights directly from their customers, users, or audiences.** The platform positions itself as a modern alternative to traditional survey tools by emphasizing authenticity, customization, and actionable responses. Its core promise is to help brands understand what people think and feel about their products or services—without relying on automated bots or generic data.

**One of its key strengths is its focus on real human feedback, real-time engagement, and anonymous response options.** Users can collect feedback from actual individuals rather than automated systems, which increases credibility and relevance. The platform also supports anonymous submissions, encouraging honesty from respondents who might otherwise hesitate to speak openly. Additionally, real-time notifications allow businesses to react quickly when feedback is submitted.

**SendFeedbacks also enables users to manage multiple brands, products, or services within one account.** This is useful for startups, agencies, or companies with multiple offerings. Each brand profile can have custom feedback forms with personalized logos, colors, and questions. These forms can then be shared through different channels, making it easy to gather feedback from various touchpoints, both internally and externally.

**Beyond collection, the platform emphasizes taking action on the responses received.** Users can respond directly to feedback, track recurring issues, and implement improvements over time. SendFeedbacks markets itself not just as a tool for gathering data, but as a system for closing the feedback loop and monitoring impact through continuous updates and insights.
